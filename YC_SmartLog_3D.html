4. Interactive 3D container loading planner

Tap a coloured cargo box to select it. Drag cargo to move it. Drag empty space to orbit the camera; pinch with two fingers to zoom. Use Rotate, Height and position controls for precise placement. Each package is independently movable, including large batches of 400–500 loose cartons. Use Auto Arrange for a layered draft, then move cartons manually. Use Fill empty spaces to repack all cartons around your locked manual positions or to optimise the entire layout. Red outline = overlap or outside container. All dimensions are to scale.

Normal cargo may support other cargo; Self Stacking can support only cartons from the same cargo entry; Top Loading Only prefers suitable supporting cargo, but may use floor space when no supported position fits; nothing above; Standalone stays on the floor with nothing above. Normal and Self Stacking cargo may be placed on the floor. Self Stacking is a geometric rule only, not a load-bearing check. Fill empty spaces searches remaining floor gaps for normal, self-stacking and standalone cargo even when no stacking surface is available. Top Loading Only falls back to floor when no suitable supported position fits. Fill empty spaces preserves cartons you lock using the Lock selected button; all unlocked cartons can be repositioned. Geometric packing is heuristic and may leave usable gaps. For 400–500 loose cartons, each carton is a separate movable box. Select by carton number; the buttons show 60 at a time for tablet performance.

Tip: lock cartons you have positioned manually, then tap Fill empty spaces to arrange the remaining cartons around them.
